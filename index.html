Who I am

Scientist.
Entrepreneur.
Problem solver.

I'm a materials scientist specialized in glass and glass-ceramic systems, with a career spanning academic research, industrial quality management, and sustainable technology entrepreneurship.

My research focuses on nucleation and crystallization of glasses, photocatalytic glass-ceramics for water treatment, and functional ceramic materials. PhD from the University of São Paulo, with postdoctoral work at UFSCar (Brazil) and the University of Nariño (Colombia).

Beyond the lab, I co-founded GAIA GreenTech — a circular economy startup for responsible management of electronic waste — and have led FAPESP-funded projects bridging materials science with environmental impact.
